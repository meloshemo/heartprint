Couples quiz: how well does your partner know you?

When you say "nothing is wrong", do they know whether anything is wrong? Do they know what softens you after a fight, how your jealousy shows, or what you actually want to hear afterwards? That is what the couples quiz asks.

You mark your answers, your partner guesses. At the end you both see the score and exactly which questions they missed. For most couples the wrong answers are the fun part.

Frequently asked

Who sees the result?

Your partner sees their own score when they finish. You see their score and the questions they missed under "My tests".

Is it free?

Yes. Creating a quiz, sending it and seeing the result are all free.

Do I need an account?

No. No email or phone number. Type your name and start.

Does the other person need to install an app?

No. The link opens in their phone browser and they take the quiz right there.

Will I know when someone finishes?

In the Android app you get a notification the moment someone finishes your quiz. On the web you see results under "My tests".
